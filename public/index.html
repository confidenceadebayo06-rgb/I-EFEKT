Safety & Digital Wellbeing

AI literacy & content authenticity

Learn to recognize synthetic media. Labels and visual clues are not proof; check the source, context and provenance before sharing.

Look for disclosure

Check for an AI-generated or AI-edited label and creator disclosure.

Verify the source

Compare with trusted sources; reverse-search suspicious images where possible.

Watch for manipulation

Look for inconsistent lighting, lip-sync, hands, reflections or unnatural audio—but these clues can be wrong.

Pause before sharing

Do not treat AI detectors as definitive. Report deceptive content for review.
